## Standards & Specifications

- **CycloneDX v1.6 AI-BOM Specification**: OWASP standard for Machine Learning Model Cards and Artificial Intelligence Software Bill of Materials.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI 100-1)**: Function `MAP` — Categorization and contextual understanding of AI system components and dependencies.
- **EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)**:
  - Article 53: Technical documentation and transparency obligations for general-purpose AI models.
  - Annex IV: Technical documentation requirements for high-risk AI architectures.
